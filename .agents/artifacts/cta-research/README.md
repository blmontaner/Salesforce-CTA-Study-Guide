## Main Conclusions

Use SF Toolkit selectively for quick reference in the sampled domains, the repository for its topic map and public practice materials, and current official Salesforce documentation to settle facts. Neither community guide is a complete or reliably correct standalone CTA curriculum.

Important findings include incorrect JWT signing instructions and unsafe private-key debugging guidance in SF Toolkit, incorrect Metadata-API-only CDC configuration claims, contradictory sharing limits, and historical backup/archive advice. The GitHub guide has verified IAM, governor-limit, sharing, integration, object-model, and privacy errors, along with incomplete chapters. The reports distinguish errors from omissions, opinions, and unresolved claims.

## Official Exam Sources

- [Evaluation guide](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=005299002&type=1): Application Architect and System Architect prerequisites; 60-minute preparation, 30-minute presentation, 30-minute Q&A, and 15-minute feedback; external camera and provided digital materials; no AI/templates.
- [Review Board guide](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=005298996&type=1&language=en_US): a passed Evaluation prerequisite; 180-minute preparation, 45-minute presentation, and 40-minute Q&A; provided digital materials and no Lucidchart templates.

Published segment timings are not verified total appointment durations. Fees, retake rules, evaluation-pass validity, and some day-of logistics remain unverified. Recheck current candidate instructions before booking.

## Scope and Maintenance

The wiki review and SF Toolkit sampling have different scopes. Embedded diagrams, practice-scenario solutions and distribution rights, and commercial entitlements were not exhaustively verified. Sources and platform limits can change after the review date. Use the reports' evidence status and product/release qualifications rather than treating every linked page or marketing SKU as universal proof.

The study-hour recommendations are planning and coaching guidance, not Salesforce requirements. Personalize them to experience, credentials, reviewer access, available hours, and whether the goal is general architecture learning, Evaluation, or Review Board.

These reports do not modify or synchronize the GitHub wiki, fix the existing guide chapters, or update this fork to the newer upstream Docusaurus site. Keep those as separate, reviewed changes. Local conversation records, machine paths, credentials, and unrelated workspace artifacts are intentionally excluded.
